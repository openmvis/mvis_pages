Monado MOC (Samsung Odyssey+) results

The MOC calibration recordings of the Monado SLAM Datasets, made with a Samsung Odyssey+ headset: its two tracking cameras and its IMU, 4 camera-only and 4 camera + IMU recordings.

2 tracking camerasheadset IMU4 + 4 recordingsAprilGrid 6×6, 30 mm

Separate study · batch 2026-11-25 · not part of the leaderboard batch

Dataset page: downloadsDevice wiki: the deviceHugging Face: the data

Repeatability, not accuracy. There is no ground truth: a small spread shows that a tool repeats, not that it is right, and a difference shows that two tools disagree, not which one is wrong. We ran every tool ourselves, including Kalibr and Basalt.

MOC-cam · camera onlyMOC · camera + IMU

Notes

Section titled “Notes”
  • Chart centre. Each chart is centred on the Monado authors' own Basalt calibration of this headset, made from one recording: extras/calibration.raw.json for the camera–IMU distance, baseline and time offset, and its kb4 version (calibration.kb4.json) for the intrinsics. Their IMU terms are not used: the released recordings appear to carry the authors' IMU correction already, so those charts, and t_ga, are centred on the MVIS median.
