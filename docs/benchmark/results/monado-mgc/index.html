Monado MGC (HP Reverb G2) results

The MGC calibration recordings of the Monado SLAM Datasets, made with an HP Reverb G2 headset: its 4 tracking cameras, recorded as three pairs (cam0 + cam1, cam0 + cam2, cam1 + cam3), and its IMU.

4 headset cameras, in 3 pairsheadset IMU4 + 4 recordings per pairAprilGrid 6×6, 30 mm

Batch 2026-11-24a (T5 and T12; the pair 02 and 13 families are separate runs of 2026-11-27) · results commit 41ec486 · Leaderboard · Methodology

Example: how MVIS calibrates itDataset page: downloadsDevice wiki: the deviceHugging Face: the data

Repeatability, not accuracy. There is no ground truth: a small spread shows that a tool repeats, not that it is right, and a difference shows that two tools disagree, not which one is wrong. We ran every tool ourselves, including Kalibr and Basalt.

T5 · camera onlyT12 · camera + IMUMGC02-cam · camera onlyMGC02 · camera + IMUMGC13-cam · camera onlyMGC13 · camera + IMU

Notes

Section titled “Notes”
  • Three camera pairs. Each recording shows the board to one pair of cameras. T5 and T12 are pair 01 (cam0 + cam1) and belong to the leaderboard batch. Pairs 02 (cam0 + cam2) and 13 (cam1 + cam3) are from separate runs of 2026-11-27 and have no row on the Leaderboard. A camera that is in two pairs has one set of values per pair.
  • Kalibr on pairs 02 and 13. We ran Kalibr camera + IMU as on pair 01 and did not tune it. It did not give a usable solve on any of the 8 recordings, so all its values there are shown faded.

Outside the leaderboard batch: MGC02-cam, MGC02, MGC13-cam and MGC13 are from separate runs, 2026-11-27 (MVIS 0.4.1, development version of 2026-11-26), added to this page beside the families of batch 2026-11-24a. They have no row on the Leaderboard and no ◆ counts.

MGC02-cam · camera only · pair 02

Section titled “MGC02-cam · camera only · pair 02”

cam0 + cam2 stereo · pair 02 · 4 recordings

Separate runs · batch 2026-11-27 · MVIS 0.4.1, development version of 2026-11-26 · not in the leaderboard batch

Read first: Kalibr and Basalt calibrate both cameras and their baseline from the images alone, on the 4 camcalib02 recordings. MVIS calibrates each camera alone. In these per-camera solves MVIS keeps every detected corner under a robust loss and has no outlier-removal pass, which its pair 01 solve has. On MGC09, cam2, 21 of 5996 corners are off by more than 3 px, up to 29 px, so the rms error is 1.04 px while the median is 0.11 px. They are single corners spread over the recording, not one bad frame, and why they are misplaced is not established.

MVIS: solves each camera of this pair alone here, so it has no baseline. Kalibr and Basalt solve the pair together.

MGC02 · camera + IMU · pair 02

Section titled “MGC02 · camera + IMU · pair 02”

cam0 + cam2 + IMU · pair 02 · 4 recordings

Separate runs · batch 2026-11-27 · MVIS 0.4.1, development version of 2026-11-26 · not in the leaderboard batch

Read first: MVIS starts from its per-camera results of the same round and refines the intrinsics. Kalibr's intrinsics and baselines are inputs from an MVIS export; its td, lever and IMU intrinsics are solved given them.

Gravity: MVIS solves at the local gravity of Córdoba, 9.7931 m/s² (0.17 % below 9.81). Kalibr uses its fixed 9.80655 m/s². Basalt is held at 9.81 m/s².

Kalibr: 4 of 4 solves are poor, shown faded below. Its IMU scales are far from 1 on every recording: gyro scale 1.2 to 12, accelerometer scale z 1.2 to 7860.
