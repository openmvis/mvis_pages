Monado MIC (Valve Index) results

The MIC calibration recordings of the Monado SLAM Datasets, made with a Valve Index headset: its front fisheye pair and its IMU, 8 camera-only and 8 camera + IMU recordings.

front fisheye pairheadset IMU8 + 8 recordingsAprilGrid 6×6, 30 mm

Separate study · batch 2026-11-27 (the camera + IMU solves of Kalibr and Basalt are from October 2026) · not part of the leaderboard batch

Dataset page: downloadsDevice wiki: the deviceHugging Face: the data

Repeatability, not accuracy. There is no ground truth: a small spread shows that a tool repeats, not that it is right, and a difference shows that two tools disagree, not which one is wrong. We ran MVIS on every recording, and Kalibr and Basalt on the camera-only ones, on 2026-11-27. The camera + IMU solves of Kalibr and Basalt are from October 2026; the Kalibr used there starts its spline from both cameras.

MIC-cam · camera onlyMIC · camera + IMU

Notes

Section titled “Notes”
  • Runs. We ran MVIS 0.4.1 (development version of 2026-11-26) on all 16 recordings, and Kalibr and Basalt on the 8 camera-only recordings, on 2026-11-27. The camera + IMU solves of Kalibr and Basalt are from October 2026. That Kalibr run uses its scale-misalignment IMU model, with a change that starts its spline from both cameras.
  • Accelerometer scale. On the camera + IMU recordings MVIS and Kalibr give a median accelerometer scale of 1.055 to 1.066 on the three axes, and Basalt 1.017 to 1.024. The accelerometer file every tool reads here (imu0/data.csv) equals the dataset's raw file with the correction of the authors' calibration file applied twice. At rest it reads 9.22 to 9.24 m/s², against a local gravity of 9.79, so a scale near 1.06 is expected. MVIS and Kalibr keep the size of gravity fixed, so their scale takes all of it. Basalt also estimates gravity and finds 9.10 to 9.62 m/s², so its scale takes only part. We did not re-run a tool on the raw file to confirm this.
  • Chart centre. Each chart is centred on the Monado authors' own Basalt calibration of this headset, made from one recording: extras/calibration.raw.json for the camera–IMU distance, baseline and time offset, and its kb4 version (calibration.json) for the intrinsics. Their IMU terms are not used: the released recordings appear to carry the authors' IMU correction already, so those charts, and t_ga, are centred on the MVIS median.

MIC · camera + IMU

Section titled “MIC · camera + IMU”

cam0 + cam1 + IMU · 8 recordings

Read first: MVIS starts from its camera-only result of the same round and refines the intrinsics. Kalibr's intrinsics and baselines are inputs from its own camera calibration on the same camera + IMU recording; its td, lever and IMU intrinsics are solved given them.

Gravity: MVIS solves at the local gravity of Córdoba, 9.7931 m/s² (0.17 % below 9.81). Kalibr uses its fixed 9.80655 m/s². Basalt estimates gravity in these runs.
