VI-Rig: 4 IMUs + 3 cameras results

Our rig: a FLIR Blackfly and a RealSense T265 fisheye pair, all global shutter, with four IMUs from 100 to 500 Hz.

our data 3 global-shutter cameras4 IMUs, 100–500 Hz

Batch 2026-11-24a · results commit 41ec486 · Leaderboard · Methodology

Dataset page: downloadsHugging Face: the data

Repeatability, not accuracy. There is no ground truth: a small spread shows that a tool repeats, not that it is right, and a difference shows that two tools disagree, not which one is wrong. We ran every tool ourselves, including Kalibr and Basalt.

T6 · camera + IMUT6c · camera only

T6 · camera + IMU

Section titled “T6 · camera + IMU”

3 GS cameras + 4 IMUs · 10 recordings

Read first: Kalibr's intrinsics and baselines are inputs from its own camera-only calibration; its td and lever are solved given them. Data settings differ between the tools.

Coverage

Section titled “Coverage”
  • MVIS 10 of 10
  • Kalibr 10 of 10
  • Basalt 0 of 10 not run: one IMU per run; no run holds the whole rig

For a fair comparison, MVIS is run here with Kalibr's IMU model, so its gyro and accel terms mean the same as Kalibr's. MVIS still estimates t_ga and refines the camera intrinsics.

Records behind these numbers: MVIS T6.json · Kalibr T6.json · Basalt T6.json. In the MVIS file, this run is the method "MVIS (Kalibr IMU model)".
