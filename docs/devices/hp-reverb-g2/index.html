Demo and values

Section titled “Demo and values”

VR headset from HP with four 640×480 grayscale tracking cameras and an IMU at about 1 kHz. All four are solved from the Monado SLAM calibration data, in three pair recordings stitched together.

Reprojection median0.13 px
cam0–cam1 baseline108.5 mm
cam0 td−1.22 ms
cam0 lever arm53.9 mm

Reprojection is how far, in pixels, the detected corners lie from where the solve puts them. td is the camera–IMU time offset, and the lever arm is the camera–IMU position offset.

Three pair recordings, with no independent reference on this page. These values show what MVIS solved, not how close they are to the truth.

Calibration process

Section titled “Calibration process”

The path of the rig around the board, as MVIS solved it. To explore it:

  • Press Play or drag the bar to follow the path.
  • Use the view buttons to change the view.
  • Pick a camera card to put that camera on the frustum, the pyramid that shows its view.

Each card draws the board from the solved pose, with the corners MVIS detected. We publish no camera images.

Limits

Section titled “Limits”
  • Three pair recordings, with no independent reference on this page. These values show what MVIS solved, not how close they are to the truth.
  • This rig is stitched from three solves, not one joint solve. Cameras seen in two pairs agree to 0.89 mm and 0.34° (cam0) and 0.54 mm and 0.15° (cam1). cam2 and cam3 move 0.79 and 0.52 mm depending on how they are attached.
  • The process figure and values for the IMU show the cam0 + cam1 recording only.

How it was run

Section titled “How it was run”
  • Each recording shows the board to one camera pair, so three pairs are solved: cam0 + cam1, cam0 + cam2, cam1 + cam3. Each is camera + IMU with camera intrinsics free and t_ga estimated, on current MVIS, 2026-11-03. cam0, cam1 and all IMU values come from the first pair. cam2 is attached through cam0 and cam3 through cam1.
