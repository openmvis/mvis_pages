Demo and values

Section titled “Demo and values”

Small VIO module from RoboBaton, 8.7 g on a 40 × 20 mm board, with one global-shutter fisheye camera and a TDK ICM-42670-P IMU, hardware-triggered. One hand-held recording in front of an AprilGrid shown on a monitor, recorded by the module's own capture tool and solved as camera + IMU.

Reprojection median0.41 px
cam0 td+1.14 ms
cam0 lever arm31.1 mm

Reprojection is how far, in pixels, the detected corners lie from where the solve puts them. td is the camera–IMU time offset, and the lever arm is the camera–IMU position offset.

One recording, 5 frames per second. The maker's own calibration of the same recording agrees on the rotation (within 0.4°) and on the lever arm across the image (within 2 mm), but not along the optical axis (17 mm here against 9 mm) nor on the time offset (1.1 ms against 0.3 ms). A digital twin of the recording puts the noise on z at about 1 mm, so the gap is systematic; the image stamp convention and the tag size on the monitor are the open questions.

Calibration process

Section titled “Calibration process”

The path of the rig around the board, as MVIS solved it. To explore it:

  • Press Play or drag the bar to follow the path.
  • Use the view buttons to change the view.
  • Pick a camera card to put that camera on the frustum, the pyramid that shows its view.

Each card draws the board from the solved pose, with the corners MVIS detected. We publish no camera images.

Limits

Section titled “Limits”
  • One recording, 5 frames per second. The maker's own calibration of the same recording agrees on the rotation (within 0.4°) and on the lever arm across the image (within 2 mm), but not along the optical axis (17 mm here against 9 mm) nor on the time offset (1.1 ms against 0.3 ms). A digital twin of the recording puts the noise on z at about 1 mm, so the gap is systematic; the image stamp convention and the tag size on the monitor are the open questions.
  • The tag size, 25 mm, is the value in the capture tool's config, not a measurement on the monitor; it scales the lever arm.
  • A double-sphere model fits as well as kb4, but its focal length and xi trade off along a flat valley, so only the projection is comparable between the two.

How it was run

Section titled “How it was run”
  • Camera with the IMU in one solve. Kannala-Brandt (kb4) fisheye intrinsics seeded from a single-camera solve of the same recording and left free; the camera time offset, IMU scale and misalignment and the gyro-to-accel offset t_ga estimated. The board shape is estimated per corner. IMU noise is the ICM-42670-P datasheet value times three. Solved 2026-11-19.
