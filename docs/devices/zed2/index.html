Demo and values

Section titled “Demo and values”

Stereo depth camera from Stereolabs with two rolling-shutter cameras 120 mm apart, wide 2.1 mm lenses and a built-in IMU at 400 Hz. This is the camera of the WHUVID driving dataset from Wuhan University; both cameras and the IMU are solved together from the authors' own calibration recording, with the rolling-shutter readout estimated.

No video on this page. The values below come from the solved run.
Reprojection median0.28 px
cam0–cam1 baseline120.1 mm
cam0 td, first row−2.49 ms+4.54 ms at mid-frame
cam0 lever arm32.2 mm

Reprojection is how far, in pixels, the detected corners lie from where the solve puts them. td is the camera–IMU time offset, and the lever arm is the camera–IMU position offset.

No independent reference. The authors publish a Kalibr calibration from the same recording with a global-shutter camera model; it isn't an independent check.

Calibration process

Section titled “Calibration process”

The path of the rig around the board, as MVIS solved it. To explore it:

  • Press Play or drag the bar to follow the path.
  • Use the view buttons to change the view.
  • Pick a camera card to put that camera on the frustum, the pyramid that shows its view.

Each card draws the board from the solved pose, with the corners MVIS detected. We publish no camera images.

Limits

Section titled “Limits”
  • No independent reference. The authors publish a Kalibr calibration from the same recording with a global-shutter camera model; it isn't an independent check.
  • The time offset is given at the first image row. At mid-image, the time a global-shutter model reports, it is about 7 ms later (+4.5 ms).
  • The board is small (about 15 cm), so the image edges are covered less well and the principal point trades with the lens distortion: a Kannala–Brandt model moves it by about 10 px with no change in fit.
  • The recording runs at 19.7 Hz and the IMU at 381 Hz, below the 30 fps and 400 Hz on the sheet, probably from drops while recording.

How it was run

Section titled “How it was run”
  • Both cameras and the IMU together. Camera intrinsics (pinhole with radial-tangential distortion, as in the authors' calibration) start from a camera-only solve and stay free; rolling-shutter readout, camera time offsets, IMU scale and misalignment and the gyro-to-accel offset t_ga are estimated. IMU noise from the authors' IMU file. Solved 2026-11-16.
