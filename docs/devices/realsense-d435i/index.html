Demo and values

Section titled “Demo and values”

Depth camera from RealSense with a 50 mm global-shutter infrared stereo pair, a rolling-shutter RGB camera and a 6-axis IMU. Three hand-held recordings in front of an AprilGrid, each solved as RGB + IMU with the rolling-shutter readout estimated.

Reprojection is how far, in pixels, the detected corners lie from where the solve puts them. td is the camera–IMU time offset, and the lever arm is the camera–IMU position offset.

The three recordings agree on the time offset (6.00 to 6.12 ms), the readout time (32.5 ms) and the lever arm across the image (within 0.4 and 1.4 mm), but not along the optical axis: z is 14.7 to 20.4 mm, against 11.3 mm in the RealSense SDK's design values. All three recordings turn the camera little about the two axes across the image, which is what makes that distance observable. Holding z at the SDK value fits nearly as well (within about 2 σ), so the SDK value is the safer choice for now.

Calibration process

Section titled “Calibration process”

The path of the rig around the board, as MVIS solved it. To explore it:

  • Press Play or drag the bar to follow the path.
  • Use the view buttons to change the view.
  • Pick a camera card to put that camera on the frustum, the pyramid that shows its view.

Each card draws the board from the solved pose, with the corners MVIS detected. We publish no camera images.

Limits

Section titled “Limits”
  • The three recordings agree on the time offset (6.00 to 6.12 ms), the readout time (32.5 ms) and the lever arm across the image (within 0.4 and 1.4 mm), but not along the optical axis: z is 14.7 to 20.4 mm, against 11.3 mm in the RealSense SDK's design values. All three recordings turn the camera little about the two axes across the image, which is what makes that distance observable. Holding z at the SDK value fits nearly as well (within about 2 σ), so the SDK value is the safer choice for now.
  • The tag size, 88 mm, is the stated size of the printed board, and it scales every lever arm.
  • The board is not flat: its corners lie up to about 6 mm out of plane, the same shape in every recording.
  • The camera stores no IMU correction, so the IMU scale and bias values are those of the raw sensor.

How it was run

Section titled “How it was run”
  • RGB camera with the IMU in one solve per recording, as a rolling-shutter camera with the readout time estimated. Pinhole camera with radial-tangential distortion, seeded from a single-camera solve of the same recording and left free; the camera time offset, IMU scale and misalignment and the gyro-to-accel offset t_ga estimated. The board shape is estimated per corner. IMU noise is the MVIS default, since no measured values exist for this unit. Solved 2026-11-14.
