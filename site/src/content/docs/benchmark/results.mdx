  <summary>How to read a cell</summary>
  <ul>
    <li>Each repeatability cell is one family and one group of parameters, compared on the recordings every compared tool ran. Its shape names the tool whose estimates scatter least; two shapes are a tie between them, both ahead of the third.</li>

## About these results

How each cell is decided: see [Repeatability verdicts](/benchmark/methodology/#repeatability-verdicts) on Methodology.

<details class="not-content rp-fold">
<summary>◆ differences between tools</summary>

The ◆ columns count, per tool pair, the parameter rows where the tools' medians differ. No tool is the reference. Each results page lists every row.

- **Disagreement:** two tools disagree where their medians differ by more than either tool's scatter.
- **◆** marks disagreement, not an error in either tool.
- **‡** on a time offset: MVIS splits the gyro (td) and accel (td + t_ga) timelines, so Basalt − Kalibr is the like-for-like check there. Here td is the camera–IMU time offset, and t_ga is the time offset between the gyroscope and accelerometer channels.

</details>
