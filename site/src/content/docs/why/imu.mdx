This page shows what an IMU calibration gains visual-inertial odometry downstream.

<WhyImuData />

**The answer first:** correct the IMU before the odometry runs; the odometry's own online calibration does not make
up for it.

<WhyImuAnswer />

How to turn it on: estimate the IMU intrinsics in [Camera–IMU calibration](/guides/camera-imu-calibration/#3-turn-on-imu-intrinsics),
then write a corrected recording with [`mvis apply-calib`](/guides/roundtrip/#export-a-corrected-dataset).

## The setup

We ran visual-inertial odometry twice on each recording, once on the original data and once on the
same data corrected by MVIS. Nothing else changes between the two runs, so any difference comes from the IMU and timing correction. We ran mono and
stereo, and used
[OpenVINS](https://docs.openvins.com/); any visual-inertial odometry would serve.

<WhyImuSetup />

Each run is scored by its position error (ATE) against the ground truth, in mm. Lower is better.

## 1. The IMU gives the odometry its metric scale

- **Metres come from the IMU:** a single camera sees motion only up to scale. The odometry takes metres from the
  accelerometer, and orientation between frames from the gyro.
- **Errors grow between camera frames:** the odometry integrates the IMU, and each camera frame corrects it.

<WhyImuErrors />

- **Why the odometry cannot fix it on its own:** the accelerometer always measures gravity. A 1 % scale error
  offsets that reading by about 0.1 m/s², and the offset changes every time the device turns, because gravity
  falls on a different axis. The odometry models the bias as drifting slowly, so it cannot follow these jumps;
  the residual error ends up in the trajectory.
<WhyImuTake>An IMU error grows between camera frames, and the odometry's bias estimate cannot absorb a scale error.</WhyImuTake>

## 2. Correcting the IMU makes mono odometry dependable

These headsets have such errors: their accelerometers read about {da('valve_index')} % low (Valve Index) and
{wmr} % low (Reverb G2, Odyssey+), the same on every recording. Removing them changes mono odometry a lot:

<WhyImuCards cam="mono" />

- **More accurate:** the median mono error drops from {mm(M.main.ate.baseline)} to {mm(M.main.ate.treatment)} mm, and
  {M.main.ate.wins} of {M.main.ate.n} recordings improve.
- **More consistent:** the worst run drops from {mm(Math.max(...kept.map((r) => r.raw)))} to
  {mm(Math.max(...kept.map((r) => r.corrected)))} mm.{bothOff.length > 0 ? ` One recording, ${bothOff.join(', ')}, fails either way.` : ''}
- **Stereo gains less:** {mm(S.main.ate.baseline)} → {mm(S.main.ate.treatment)} mm. Two cameras give their own
  scale and hide part of the IMU error, so mono, which leans on the IMU most, gains most.

<WhyImuTake>Corrected, mono error falls from {mm(M.main.ate.baseline)} to {mm(M.main.ate.treatment)} mm, and the spread across recordings shrinks.</WhyImuTake>

## 3. Online calibration does not catch up

Visual-inertial odometry can calibrate the camera time offset $t_d$ and the IMU intrinsics online, while it runs. Can that
replace the offline correction? We turned it on for the original data and compared with the MVIS-corrected data, where nothing
runs online.

<WhyImuOnline cam="mono" />

- **It helps, but stays behind:** the most accurate setting, {lowest(M).label}, reaches {mm(lowest(M).baseline)} mm against
  {mm(lowest(M).treatment)} mm for the MVIS-corrected data, which is more accurate on {lowest(M).wins} of {lowest(M).n}.
- **Even with the best setting per recording:** give each recording whichever online setting was most accurate there, and the MVIS-corrected
  data still wins on {M.beats_every_online.k} of {M.beats_every_online.n} (stereo: {S.beats_every_online.k} of
  {S.beats_every_online.n}).
- **Why:** online, the odometry gets only {Math.round(Math.min(...reached) * 100)}–{Math.round(Math.max(...reached) * 100)} % of the way to the
  MVIS accelerometer scale, and it has no gyro–accel offset $t_{ga}$ at all ([Why calibrate t_ga](/why/tga/)).
- **On top of the correction it adds nothing:** online makes the MVIS-corrected data more accurate on only
  {onc(M, 'imu_td').wins} of {onc(M, 'imu_td').n} (mono) and {onc(S, 'imu_td').wins} of {onc(S, 'imu_td').n}
  (stereo).

<WhyImuTake>Calibrate offline, correct the recording, and leave the odometry's online calibration off.</WhyImuTake>

## 5. The same error on VI-Rig

Our own rig shows it too. In the [VI-Rig 4 IMUs + 4 cameras results](/benchmark/results/virig-4imus-4cams/#T8)
(15 recordings), MVIS finds the T265 IMU's accelerometer 1.7–2.0 % off on every axis, while the Xsens on the same
rig stays within 0.1 %.
