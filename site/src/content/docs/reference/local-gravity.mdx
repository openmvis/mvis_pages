A camera + IMU solve holds the gravity magnitude fixed. Gravity is most of what an
accelerometer measures, so the accelerometer scale (`Da`) takes up any error in it.
Give MVIS the place where the data was recorded:

```yaml title="cam_imu.yaml"
site: {latitude_deg: 22.54, altitude_m: 20, name: "Shenzhen"}   # name is only a label
```

- **Without it:** MVIS uses 9.81 m/s² and the run log warns. The accelerometer scale
  then reads off by the ratio g / 9.81: +0.23 % in Shenzhen, +0.30 % at the equator,
  close to zero in central Europe.
- **A measured value** from a gravity survey goes in `gravity:` (m/s²) instead.
- **Coordinates, not a city name:** names repeat (Córdoba, Spain and Córdoba, Argentina),
  and a site outside any city still has coordinates from a phone or a map.
- **Precision:** anywhere in the right city is enough. One degree of latitude moves g by
  at most 0.01 %, 100 m of altitude by 0.003 %. Altitude matters only for high places
  (Kunming −0.06 %, Lhasa −0.11 %).

Requires MVIS 0.4.0 or later. The keys and their checks are in
[Configuration](/reference/configuration/).
